SMS Consent

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Codex SMS Bridge

Aggrow operates a private SMS bridge for the account owner to send commands to a personal Codex assistant and receive automated replies. This is not a marketing program, and messages are only sent in response to requests from the authorized user.

Opt-In

To opt in, the authorized user texts START to +1 833-578-1005 from their own mobile number. By texting START, the user consents to receive automated SMS replies from the Codex SMS bridge.

Message Terms

Message frequency varies based on user requests. Message and data rates may apply. The service only responds to the allowlisted owner number.

Opt-Out and Help

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help, or email adam@aggrow.net.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS content is used only to operate and secure the private Codex SMS bridge.
